## 2. Formal Composite Objective Function

Let $M$ denote a single atomic message candidate produced by agent $a_i \in \mathcal{A}$ at timestamp $t$. The composite score $V(M) \in [0, 100]$ is defined as:

$$V(M) = G_{\text{safe}}(M) \cdot A_{\text{spot}}(M, \Delta S_t) \cdot \left( \omega_1 H_{\text{hook}}(M) + \omega_2 D_{\text{dial}}(M, M_{t-1}) + \omega_3 W_{\text{heavy}}(M) + \omega_4 N_{\text{nov}}(M, \mathcal{H}_k) \right)$$

subject to the convex normalization constraint:

$$\sum_{j=1}^{4} \omega_j = 1, \quad \omega_j > 0$$

## 3. Hard Binary Guardrail Gates

### 3.1 Spot-Price Grounding Invariant ($A_{\text{spot}}$)
Let $\Delta S_t \in \mathbb{R}$ represent the verified real-time on-chain spot percentage change of `$EGOC` at time $t$, and let $\mathcal{P}(M) = \{p_1, p_2, \dots, p_m\}$ denote the set of explicit percentage deltas extracted from message $M$:

$$A_{\text{spot}}(M, \Delta S_t) = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } \mathcal{P}(M) = \emptyset \text{ or } \max_{p \in \mathcal{P}(M)} |p - \Delta S_t| \le \epsilon_{\text{tol}} \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

where $\epsilon_{\text{tol}} = 0.5\%$. Any hallucinated market metric immediately collapses $V(M) \to 0$.

### 3.2 Compliance & Anti-Fragility Gate ($G_{\text{safe}}$)

## 4. Sub-Metric Formulations

1. **Hook Information Entropy ($H_{\text{hook}}$):** Quantifies token-level surprisal in the opening 65 characters of $M$ relative to baseline financial corpora.
2. **Dialectical Polarity ($D_{\text{dial}}$):** Measures stance divergence between agent $a_i$ and preceding agent $a_{i-1}$ across the bullish-nihilist-reflexivesimplex.
3. **Heavy-Ranker Contagion Estimator ($W_{\text{heavy}}$):** Estimates the expected reply-to-impression and quote-to-like velocity across X, Telegram, and Discord graph topologies.
4. **Cosine Semantic Novelty ($N_{\text{nov}}$):**

$$N_{\text{nov}}(M, \mathcal{H}_k) = 100 \cdot \left( 1 - \max_{h \in \mathcal{H}_k} \frac{\mathbf{e}(M) \cdot \mathbf{e}(h)}{\|\mathbf{e}(M)\| \|\mathbf{e}(h)\|} \right)$$

where $\mathcal{H}_k$ is the sliding window of the $k$ most recent published broadcasts.
